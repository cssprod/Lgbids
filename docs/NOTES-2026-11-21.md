# Why v1 differs from the uploaded plan (read when you are back)

The uploaded plan described a Python + Playwright + CustomTkinter desktop scanner. Lgbids already
does more than that (scoring, document reading, drafting, sending, PWA), so this session built
the gaps the plan pointed at, inside the existing app, and changed these points:

1. **No bot-check or robots.txt workarounds.** The plan suggested `--disable-blink-features=
   AutomationControlled` and a visible browser to get past BC Bid's captcha. That breaks the sites'
   terms and the project's recorded decision. BC Bid and Bonfire bids come in through their own
   alert e-mails instead (already built), and that keeps working when the sites change their pages.
2. **Web app + launcher instead of a PyInstaller desktop app.** One codebase serves desktop and
   phone (installable PWA). `Start Lgbids.bat` / `.command` / `start-lgbids.sh` give the
   double-click start. Nothing to sign, no bundled 150 MB Chromium, no SmartScreen warnings.
3. **ntfy next to Telegram, no carrier SMS or Twilio.** ntfy needs no account or bot set-up (install
   the app, pick a topic). Carrier gateways are unreliable in Canada, as the plan itself noted.
4. **Secrets stored like the SMTP password (write-only API), not the OS keyring.** The app is a
   server that can run headless or on a VPS, where there is no desktop keyring.
5. **The new municipal sources start off.** This sandbox cannot reach those sites, so their readers
   are unverified. Press Test on the Sources page from your computer, then turn them on.
   The ones that work stay on after restarts. Sources blocked by robots.txt are forced off.
6. **"Layout may have changed" warning per source instead of "0 rows overall".** A single total
   hides one broken site behind the others. Each source is checked against its own last good run.
7. **No LLM filter.** The rule-based relevance and area scoring already separates "lawnmower
   purchase" from "grounds maintenance", with reasons shown. An optional AI helper is in v4 of
   docs/ROADMAP.md.
8. **CSV guarded against formula injection.** Bid titles come from outside, and a title starting
   with `=` could run as a formula in Excel. Those cells get a leading `'`.
9. **Not done here:** the live check of the new sources (needs your network) and real alert-e-mail
   samples. Both are listed first in v2.
